# notetag

* [HP Cost](notetags/hp-cost.md) - The tags making a skill cost HP to execute, by a flat amount, a percentage of max HP, or a formula.
* [HP Cost Reduction](notetags/hp-cost-reduction.md) - The tag reducing how much HP skills cost, as percentage points subtracted from 100 (hcr).
* [HP Gain](notetags/hp-gain.md) - The tags restoring HP to the caster when a skill is executed, by a flat amount, a percentage, or a formula.
* [Extra MP and TP Costs and Gains](notetags/mp-tp-costs-and-gains.md) - The flat, percent and formula tags for MP and TP costs and gains, layered on the editor's cost fields.
* [Stack Cost and Item Cost](notetags/stack-and-item-cost.md) - The tags making a skill cost state stacks or inventory items to cast, refused like insufficient MP.
* [Natural Growth](notetags/natural-growth.md) - The integration details with the J-NaturalGrowth plugin, buffing and growing life cost (hcr).
