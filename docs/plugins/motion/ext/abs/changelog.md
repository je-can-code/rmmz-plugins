# Changelog

All the versioning and history of changes live here.

## The Actual Changelog

### 1.2.1
  - Kept the loot expiry warning working with J-ABS 4.26.0.
### 1.2.0
  - Enemies now fold into view when their page brings them onto the map, and fold away when it takes them off, instead
    of popping in and out. A folding enemy can't be hit on the way out, and doesn't act until it is facing you.
### 1.1.0
  - A loot drop about to expire now blinks, then dissolves over its closing frames, so it stops vanishing without
    warning. Loot being drawn toward somebody is exempt, and the pacing is configured in data/config.motion.json.
### 1.0.0
  - The initial release.
