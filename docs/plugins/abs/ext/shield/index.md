# notetag

* [Shielding](notetags/shielding.md) - The tag defining how many shield points a state supplies to protect against damage.
* [Shield Amplification / Effectiveness](notetags/shield-amplification-effectiveness.md) - The tags scaling the shields a battler grants (SAR) and the shields a battler receives (SER).
* [Shield Caps](notetags/shield-caps.md) - The tag defining the most shield points a state's shield can grow to when it is reapplied.
* [Shield Priority](notetags/shield-priority.md) - The tag ordering which of a battler's shields is consumed first.
* [Shield Protect](notetags/shield-protect.md) - The tag making a shield nullify the overflow damage of the blow that breaks it.
* [Shield Type](notetags/shield-type.md) - The tag limiting a shield to soaking damage of specific elements.
* [Shield Bypass](notetags/shield-bypass.md) - The tag letting a skill ignore every shield, or only the shields of certain elements.
* [Shield Bonus Damage](notetags/shield-bonus-damage.md) - The tag adding bonus damage that a skill deals to shields.
* [Shield Break Skills](notetags/shield-break-skills.md) - The tag defining the skills a battler executes when its shield breaks.
* [Natural Growth](notetags/natural-growth.md) - The integration details with the J-NaturalGrowth plugin, growing shield amplification and effectiveness.
