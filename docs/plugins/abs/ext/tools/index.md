# notetag

* [Gap Closing](notetags/gap-closing/gap-closing.md) - The tags marking a skill as gap closing and a target as gap closable, matched by key.
* [Gap Close Any](notetags/gap-closing/gap-close-any.md) - The tag letting a skill gap close to whatever single target it hits, without matching keys.
* [Block Gap Close](notetags/gap-closing/block-gap-close.md) - The tag making a battler immune to all gap closing, including gap close any skills.
* [Gap Close Mode](notetags/gap-closing/gap-close-mode.md) - The tag choosing how the caster of a gap-closing skill travels to the resolved destination.
* [Gap Close Position](notetags/gap-closing/gap-close-position.md) - The tag choosing where, relative to the target, the caster of a gap-closing skill lands.
* [Respect Terrain](notetags/gap-closing/respect-terrain.md) - The tag cancelling a gap close when the caster cannot legally reach the destination tile.
* [On Gap Close End](notetags/gap-closing/on-gap-close-end.md) - The tags firing follow-up skills the moment a gap close arrives at its destination.
* [Pull Forward](notetags/pull-forward/pull-forward.md) - The tag pulling a skill's target toward the caster on hit, the inverse of gap close.
