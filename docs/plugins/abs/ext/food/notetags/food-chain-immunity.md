# Food Chain Immunity

## Description

Any battler whose `getAllNotes()` sources include the following tag keeps their arc through a
[chain-ending skill](ending-a-chain-metabolize.md). This tag may appear on any passive state, accessory, class, or other
note-bearing database object.

The bearer still executes the skill and still receives everything it does; they simply do not spend the meal. This is
the capstone form of food mastery — fullness stops being ammunition and becomes a standing condition.

## TAG USAGE

- Actors
- Classes
- Weapons
- Armor, such as accessories.
- States, such as passive states.
- Enemies
- Skills

`<foodChainImpervious>`
