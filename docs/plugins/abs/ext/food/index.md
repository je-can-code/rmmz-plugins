# notetag

* [Food Items](notetags/food-items.md) - The tag designating an item as food, and naming the food chain group it belongs to.
* [Food Chain States](notetags/food-chain-states.md) - The tag marking a state as one phase of a food group's chain arc.
* [Food Chain Bar Color](notetags/food-chain-bar-color.md) - The tag setting the color a food chain phase state's segment renders with in the food chain HUD bar.
* [Ending a Chain (Metabolize)](notetags/ending-a-chain-metabolize.md) - The tag making a skill end the caster's active food arc when it executes.
* [Food Chain Immunity](notetags/food-chain-immunity.md) - The tag letting a battler keep their food arc through a chain-ending skill.
