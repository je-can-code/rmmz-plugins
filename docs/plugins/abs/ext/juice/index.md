# notetag

* [Juice Icon](notetags/juice-icon.md) - The tag forcing which IconSet icon a skill's weapon swing overlay shows.
* [No Juice](notetags/no-juice.md) - The tag suppressing all juice motion on a skill's caster when the skill executes.
* [Juice Motion](notetags/juice-motion.md) - The tag selecting the preset weapon overlay or caster-body motion a skill plays when it executes.
* [Cast Motion](notetags/cast-motion.md) - The tag selecting the body motion a skill's caster shows for the whole of its cast.
* [Cast Motion Period](notetags/cast-motion-period.md) - The tag setting how many frames one repetition of a skill's cast motion takes.
* [Cast Motion Intensity](notetags/cast-motion-intensity.md) - The tag setting how far a skill's cast motion deforms the caster's sprite at its peak.
* [Juice Span](notetags/juice-span.md) - The tag setting the arc span in degrees for a skill's arc weapon overlay motions.
* [Juice Repeat Count](notetags/juice-repeat-count.md) - The tag setting how many times a skill's juice motion repeats within its duration.
* [Juice Duration](notetags/juice-duration.md) - The tag overriding how many frames a skill's weapon swing animation lasts.
* [Juice Stab Tip Degrees](notetags/juice-stab-tip-degrees.md) - The tag setting which way a skill's overlay icon points its tip or bore at rotation 0.
* [Juice Profile Gun](notetags/juice-profile-gun.md) - The tag making a skill's side-profile firearm icon mirror east/west instead of rotating about 180 degrees.
* [Juice Weapon Style](notetags/juice-weapon-style.md) - The tag selecting which tilt/swing multiplier row of the juice config a skill uses.

# plugin-command

* [Apply Overlay Plugin Command](commands/apply-overlay.md) - Shows an icon over a character in one of the overlay presets.
* [Remove Overlay Plugin Command](commands/remove-overlay.md) - Takes down whatever a source was holding up on a character.
